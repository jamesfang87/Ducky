A reasoning path · PHYS 1401

A 7 kg bowling ball and a 60 g feather drop from 2 m. Which lands first?

Hint 1 of 2 Voice required

  1. F = mg: gravity pulls the ball down with 69 N, the feather with 0.6 N.

    69 N vs 0.6 N
  2. !

    “More force, so the ball must fall faster.”

    Misconception · Mass changes fall speed

    Force isn’t acceleration. Something else grew along with the mass.

  3. a = F/m: 69/7 and 0.6/0.06 both give 9.8 m/s². The mass cancels.

    a = 9.8 m/s²

  4. Both land at t = √(2h/g) ≈ 0.64 s. In air, drag — not weight — separates them.

    t ≈ 0.64 s

Ducky: In air, gravity on the feather hasn’t changed. What else is pushing on it?
